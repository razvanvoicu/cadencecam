Delete your CadenceCam account

CadenceCam · Account and data deletion

The quickest way to delete your account is the Delete account command inside CadenceCam. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Delete it in the app

  1. Sign in to the CadenceCam mobile app or CadenceCam on the web.
  2. Open the hamburger menu (☰) and choose Delete account.
  3. Read the confirmation and choose Delete account again.

This path works for accounts created with Google, Apple, or a CadenceCam invitation.

If you cannot sign in

Email raz@raz.sg with the subject “CadenceCam account deletion”. Send the request from the email address attached to your Google or Apple account when possible. For an invitation account, include its invitation username but do not email its access code. We may ask for enough information to verify that the account is yours before deleting it.

We respond to email requests within 30 days.

What deletion removes

  • the CadenceCam account identity and every signed-in session;
  • workouts, rep counts, settings, browser signal traces, browser test results, and device-pairing records;
  • the linked Google or Apple authorisation, or the invitation’s ability to be used again.

For a deleted invitation, CadenceCam retains only its username, one-way access-code digest and disabled status so that the deleted credential cannot be reactivated.

Short-lived operational server logs are not account records and age out after 30 days, as described in the Privacy Policy.
