Privacy Policy

CadenceCam · Effective 18 September 2026

In short. We receive only your name and email address from Google. We keep a record of how you use CadenceCam — your counting sessions, rep counts and settings — so the app can work across your devices and show you your history. Usage data is deleted after 90 days. Your camera image never leaves your device. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the providers that host the service. You can view, export and delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

CadenceCam (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is provided by Raz Voicu, an individual based in Singapore. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to the app at cadencecam.raz.sg.

For any question or request about your data, write to raz@raz.sg.

2. What we collect

From your Google account

You sign in with Google. We ask Google only for your basic profile — the openid, email and profile permissions — and use from it only your name and email address. We do not access your contacts, files, calendar, photos or anything else in your Google account.

To keep you signed in, Google also gives us an authorisation token. We store it with your sign-in record, use it only to renew your session, and revoke it with Google when you sign out.

What you enter

In the app’s settings you can enter your body weight, the exercises you do, a description of the equipment, and a factor used to estimate calories. These are used only to calculate the figures shown to you.

How you use the app

  • Your counting sessions: when each started and ended, and how it ended.
  • The number of repetitions counted, and the timing of those repetitions, which the app uses to show your pace and estimate calories.
  • Which of your devices is counting, so that only one does at a time.

Your camera

The camera image is processed on your device and is never uploaded or stored by us. The app reduces each frame to four brightness values — one for each quarter of the picture — and counts repetitions from how those values change. No photo or video is recorded.

Those brightness values stay on your device unless a signal trace is captured, a diagnostic feature used to check and improve counting accuracy. A trace contains the brightness values for the recent past, the number of repetitions counted, the model of your device as reported by your browser, and the capabilities and settings your camera reports. It never contains an image.

Technical information

  • Device description — the device model and browser, as your browser reports them.
  • Connection details — your devices connect to each other directly (see section 4). To set that connection up, they exchange messages through our server which include the network addresses of your devices.
  • Server logs — like any website, our servers record requests: the address they came from, what was requested, and when. When you sign in or out, the log entry also includes your email address.
  • Test results — if you use the app’s Test mode, which measures counting accuracy against an on-screen movement, the result of each test is stored with your email address.

On your device

The app uses one cookie, which keeps you signed in. It is essential for the app to work and is not used for tracking. The app also keeps a few things in your browser’s storage: your name and email address (so the app can reappear straight away when you reload it), the current rep count (so a reload does not lose your set), and which screen you were on. We use no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking of any kind.

3. How we use it

We use your data only to:

  • sign you in and keep you signed in;
  • count your repetitions and show the count, pace and calorie estimates on your other devices;
  • keep your settings, and show you your history;
  • make sure only one of your devices counts at a time;
  • diagnose problems and improve the accuracy of counting;
  • keep the service secure and working.

We do not sell your data, rent it, use it for advertising, or use it to build a profile of you.

By signing in you consent to this use, as provided for by Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your data and no longer using the app (see section 7).

4. How your devices talk to each other

When one device counts and another shows a dashboard, the two connect directly to each other over your network. Your rep counts travel between them and do not pass through our servers. Our server’s part is only to introduce the two devices to each other, which is the exchange of connection details described in section 2.

5. Who we share it with

We do not share your data with anyone, except:

  • The providers who run the service. CadenceCam is hosted on Google Cloud: Cloud Run serves the app, Firestore stores its data, and Cloud Logging keeps the server logs. Google processes this data on our behalf and under our instructions. Google is also the provider you sign in with.
  • When the law requires it, or where it is necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of our users or others.

CadenceCam’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

6. Where it is stored, and for how long

Your data is stored on Google Cloud in Singapore (region asia-southeast1).

  • Usage data — sessions, rep counts, signal traces, test results and connection details — is deleted 90 days after it is recorded.
  • Your settings are kept until you change them or delete your data.
  • Your sign-in record, including the authorisation token, is deleted when you sign out, and otherwise when the session expires.
  • Server logs are deleted after 30 days.
  • Data on your device remains until you clear your browser’s storage. The app removes your name and email address from it when you sign out.

7. Your rights and choices

  • See your data. The app shows your history of counting sessions.
  • Take a copy. You can export your data from the app as a CSV file.
  • Correct it. You can change your settings at any time.
  • Delete it. You can delete your data from within the app, or ask us to by writing to raz@raz.sg. Deletion removes your sessions, rep counts, settings, signal traces and test results.
  • Withdraw consent. Delete your data and stop using the app. You can also remove CadenceCam’s access from your Google account at any time.

We respond to requests sent by email within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Commission.

8. How we protect it

  • All traffic between your device and the service is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Your sign-in cookie cannot be read by scripts on the page.
  • Your account’s records are filed under a keyed digest of your email address rather than the address itself.
  • Every request is checked against your sign-in, and each account can reach only its own data.
  • Your Google authorisation is revoked when you sign out.

No method of storing or transmitting data is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect yours.

9. Children

CadenceCam is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used the app, write to us and we will delete their data.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update it here and change the effective date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
